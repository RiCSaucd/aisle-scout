# Aisle Scout — one-pager

**The grocery book that believes the shelf tag.**

Compare Aldi, Walmart #579, Publix, Target, CVS, farms, and ship-it for zip **32080** (St. Augustine Beach). Scan a tag in the aisle. That price beats Walmart.com and Instacart every time.

## Problem

Website prices lie. BOGOs, Circle deals, and rollbacks live on the tag. Shoppers still bounce between five apps. Delivery memberships hide the real cost of milk.

## Product (live)

- Price book + BOGO / Circle / rollback / coupon math
- List blender and trip plan
- Camera / UPC scanner; fridge and pantry snap; expiry; cook-from-stock
- USDA Organic floor + St. Augustine farms
- Ship-it: Walmart+, Instacart, Shipt, clubs vs driving
- Official Walmart I/O lookup (store 579) and Instacart list push — **in-aisle logs always win**

Repo: [github.com/RiCSaucd/aisle-scout](https://github.com/RiCSaucd/aisle-scout)

## Why it is fundable

1. **Local density** — one zip, real stores, real tags. Expand zip by zip, not “all of grocery.”
2. **Trust wedge** — shelf > website. Competitors scrape. We refuse to.
3. **Membership math** — cheapest way to get food to the house, including the Costco/Sam’s drive.
4. **Hardware later** — Bluetooth fridge, camera, pantry. Software first.
